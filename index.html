About

I build the layer everything
else depends on.

For most of twenty years my work has sat underneath systems that people cannot afford to have fail — benefits platforms, eligibility systems, regulated public-sector cloud. When these break, someone loses their coverage. That shapes how I design.

Since 2019 I have been Lead AWS Cloud Architect on MD THINK, Maryland's shared cloud platform for human services, leading a team across DevOps, networking, identity and access management and system administration. Before that I spent nearly three years as Senior Technical Release and Infrastructure Lead on the Arkansas Enrollment and Eligibility Framework at Arkansas DHS. The through-line is the same: large, regulated, high-consequence systems, moved to the cloud without losing the guarantees they had before.

The other half of my work is research. Across nine peer-reviewed papers from 2022 to 2025 I've developed a connected set of frameworks — AI-Augmented DevSecOps, Cognitive DevOps, Neuroadaptive DevOps, Trustworthy MLOps and PromptOps — that embed predictive models and large language models directly into delivery pipelines, so infrastructure anticipates failure instead of reacting to it. I serve on two journal editorial boards, review others' work in the field, and have given keynotes at international conferences in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
